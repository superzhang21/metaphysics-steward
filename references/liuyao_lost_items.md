# Liuyao Lost Item Analysis (六爻寻物要义)

This guide provides structured logic for locating lost items using Liuyao divination.

## 1. Defining the Yong Shen (用神 - Target)
- **General Items / Wealth:** Wife/Wealth (妻财)
- **Clothing / Books / Documents:** Parents (父母)
- **Electronics / Valuables:** Wife/Wealth (妻财) or Child (子孙)
- **Daily Utilities:** Official/Ghost (官鬼 - if suspecting theft) or specific line properties.

## 2. Location Logic
### House vs. Outside
- **Inner Hexagram (Lines 1-3):** Within the current house or immediate vicinity.
- **Outer Hexagram (Lines 4-6):** Another house, office, or outside in the world.
- **Traveling Hexagram (旅卦):** Item is definitely "away" or forgotten in a different location.

### Room Mapping
- **Line 1 (Bottom):** Floor, shoes, low cabinets, ground.
- **Line 2:** Kitchen, bedroom (living space), stools.
- **Line 3:** Bed, chairs, stairs, transition areas.
- **Line 4:** Doorway, windows, walkway, middle-height desks.
- **Line 5:** High cabinets, tables, center of the room.
- **Line 6 (Top):** Ceiling, attic, roof, top shelf.

### Cardinal Directions (Ba Gua)
- **Li (South):** Bright, hot, electronics, kitchen/living room.
- **Kan (North):** Dark, cold, water, bathroom, laundry.
- **Zhen/Xun (East/SE):** Wood, plants, tall furniture, study.
- **Dui/Qian (West/NW):** Metal, sharp objects, jewelry, safe.
- **Gen/Kun (NE/SW):** Earth, stone, corners, closets, storage.

## 3. Color and Attribute Mapping (Liu Shen)
- **Xuanwu (玄武):** Black, dark blue, hidden, dark corners, "Dark Silver" (when combined with Metal).
- **Zhuque (朱雀):** Red, purple, bright colors, noisy places, fabrics.
- **Qinglong (青龙):** Green, cyan, expensive, new, wood.
- **Baihu (白虎):** White, silver, metal, hard, broken.
- **Gouteng/Sheshe:** Yellow, earth, messy, twisted, ropes/cables.

## 4. Verification Pattern (Logic Synthesizer)
When analyzing for high-value decisions, use `execute_code` to model the Ganzhi logic:
1. **Model:** Map Stems/Branches to numeric strengths.
2. **Timing:** Calculate the "Filling Date" (填实) or "Clashing Date" (冲实) for finding the item.
3. **Logic:** Cross-verify Hexagram meanings with Line positions.
